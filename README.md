## Cats

Cats are small, carnivorous mammals that have lived alongside people for
roughly 10,000 years, probably starting when they hunted rodents in early
farming settlements.

- **Senses:** They see well in low light, hear frequencies far above human
  range, and use their whiskers to judge gaps and nearby objects.
- **Sleep:** Adult cats sleep 12–16 hours a day, and are most active at dawn
  and dusk.
- **Communication:** Adult cats rarely meow at each other; meowing is mostly
  for humans. Purring, slow blinks, and tail position carry most of the
  message.
- **Landing:** The "righting reflex" lets a falling cat rotate to land feet
  first, which it can do from about seven weeks old.

A cat sitting on your keyboard during a code review is a feature, not a bug.
